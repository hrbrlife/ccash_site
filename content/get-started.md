        <h2>What Happens Next</h2>
        <p><strong>1. Tell us about your operation.</strong> Reach out through our contact form. Tell us about your business model, target currencies, expected volume, and timeline. Your inquiry opens a case in our onboarding workflow.</p>
        <p><strong>2. Onboarding and review.</strong> We complete identity verification — Know Your Customer / Know Your Business (KYC/KYB) — fit-and-proper screening, risk assessment, and entity structuring. Every step verified before the next opens.</p>
        <p><strong>3. Launch as a Client Series.</strong> Go live on the shared platform under CCASH's US FinCEN MSB registration — your brand, your clients, your operations. Graduate to your own self-hosted instance or license when you're ready.</p>
