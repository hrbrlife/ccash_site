        <h2>Pricing Philosophy</h2>
        <p>CCA.SH prices services transparently on a cost-plus basis. The client pays our actual cost for banking, custody, and processing, plus a markup that covers compliance overhead, technology investment, and margin.</p>
        <ul>
            <li><strong>Volume-Based Pricing:</strong> Larger clients with higher transaction volumes receive lower markups</li>
            <li><strong>Risk-Based Pricing:</strong> Higher-risk profiles or transaction types carry higher markups to reflect additional compliance burden</li>
        </ul>

        <h2>Fee Components</h2>
        <table>
            <thead><tr><th>Fee</th><th>Structure</th></tr></thead>
            <tbody>
                <tr><td>Transaction fees</td><td>Based on volume and risk tier</td></tr>
                <tr><td>Monthly platform fee</td><td>Infrastructure access and support</td></tr>
                <tr><td>One-time onboarding fee</td><td>Setup and integration</td></tr>
                <tr><td>IT license fee</td><td>Percentage of transaction volume</td></tr>
            </tbody>
        </table>

        <div class="concept-box">
            <h4>Wholesale Model</h4>
            <p>Default entry tier: <strong>3× the best-available correspondent routing fee</strong> for that rail. At real bulk volumes, pricing declines to a <strong>40% markup floor (1.4×)</strong>. The Whitelabel-Client's own pricing to its customers is its own decision, bounded by a "within reason" standard.</p>
        </div>

        <p>Detailed pricing is set forth in the Pricing and Routing Schedule (DOC:C3), which is attached to the Client Services Agreement and may be updated with reasonable notice.</p>
    </div>
