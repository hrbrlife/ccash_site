        <h2>The Graduation Path for PSPs</h2>
        <p>Payment Service Providers need infrastructure that handles multiple currencies, routes payments reliably, and keeps every transaction compliant. Building it from scratch is expensive. Renting a platform that owns your client relationships is risky. CCASH gives you a better way.</p>
        <p><strong>Start on the shared platform.</strong> Multi-currency wallets across fiat, stablecoin, and crypto. Payment processing with risk-tiered OTP approval gates — every transaction classified Low, Medium, or High with appropriate verification. FX rates applied at swap time.         <p><strong>Graduate when you scale.</strong> Your own self-hosted CCASH instance. Full control over your payment infrastructure. Your brand, your data, your client relationships. No per-transaction fees. No platform dependency.</p>
