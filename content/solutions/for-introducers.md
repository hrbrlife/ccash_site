        <h2>Why Introduce Clients to CCASH?</h2>
        <p>If you work with money-services businesses, payment operators, crypto platforms, or family offices, you already know their pain: building compliant infrastructure from scratch is expensive and slow. Renting locks them in. CCASH gives them a third path — and you earn revenue share for making the introduction.</p>

        <h2>How It Works</h2>
        <p><strong>1. Sign up as an introducer.</strong> Complete basic identity verification. Execute an introducer agreement.</p>
        <p><strong>2. Refer qualified operators.</strong> Introduce Money Services Businesses (MSBs), Payment Service Providers (PSPs), Virtual Asset Service Providers (VASPs), or family offices that need money-services infrastructure. Each referral is tracked to you.</p>
        <p><strong>3. CCASH handles the rest.</strong> We complete onboarding — Know Your Business (KYB) verification, risk assessment, entity structure, platform deployment, compliance training, go-live. You stay informed. Your client gets a fully operational platform.</p>
        <p><strong>4. Earn revenue share.</strong> Receive referral fees on completed onboardings. The more qualified operators you introduce, the more you earn.</p>

        <h2>Who Makes a Good Introducer</h2>
        <ul>
            <li>Corporate service providers and trust companies</li>
            <li>Law firms with fintech or payments practices</li>
            <li>Compliance consultancies serving MSBs and PSPs</li>
            <li>Accountants and auditors with money-services clients</li>
            <li>Industry networks and trade associations</li>
            <li>Anyone with relationships in payments, FX, crypto, or treasury</li>
        </ul>

        <h2>What You Don't Need</h2>
        <ul>
            <li>No MSB license required to refer</li>
            <li>No infrastructure to run</li>
            <li>No compliance obligations for the referred operator's activity</li>
            <li>No ongoing operational involvement (unless you want it)</li>
        </ul>
