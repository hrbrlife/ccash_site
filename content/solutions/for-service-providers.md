        <h2>Your Platform. Your Brand. Your Clients.</h2>
        <p>Trust companies, corporate service providers, and infrastructure operators need a platform they can offer to their own clients — under their brand, on their terms. CCASH's whitelabel model is built for exactly this.</p>

        <h2>How It Works</h2>
        <p><strong>1. Deploy your whitelabel instance.</strong> CCASH provisions a branded platform instance — your logo, your domain, your site profile. The same binary that powers CCASH, now under your brand.</p>
        <p><strong>2. Onboard your clients.</strong> Each of your clients gets their own isolated compartment — multi-currency wallets, payment processing, FX, travel cheques, and full compliance operations. All under your brand. All on your platform.</p>
        <p><strong>3. Manage with oversight.</strong> The whitelabel oversight role gives you aggregate visibility across your client base — operational metrics, queue depths, settlement times — without ever seeing end-client PII.</p>
        <p><strong>4. Scale on your terms.</strong> Deploy on shared infrastructure to start. Graduate to your own self-hosted instance when your client base grows. The platform scales with you.</p>

        <h2>What You Get</h2>
        <ul>
            <li>Fully branded platform instance — your logo, domain, and site profile</li>
            <li>Multi-currency wallets across fiat, stablecoin, and crypto</li>
            <li>Send, receive, swap, and travel-cheque operations</li>
            <li>Know Your Customer / Anti-Money Laundering (KYC/AML) review surfaces, sanctions screening verdicts, and Travel Rule handoff</li>
            <li>Risk-tiered approval gates and four-eyes control</li>
            <li>Cryptographically signed audit trail for every client</li>
            <li>Whitelabel oversight — aggregate visibility, no end-client PII exposure</li>
            <li>Path to self-hosted deployment when you're ready</li>
        </ul>

        <h2>Who This Is For</h2>
        <ul>
            <li>Trust companies and corporate service providers</li>
            <li>Family office platforms serving multiple families</li>
            <li>Compliance and infrastructure providers</li>
            <li>Industry associations building shared platforms for members</li>
            <li>Any organization that wants to offer money-services infrastructure under its own brand</li>
        </ul>

        <h2>Deployment Options</h2>
        <p><strong>Shared platform.</strong> Start on CCASH's infrastructure. Fast deployment. No infrastructure to manage. Your brand on the front, CCASH behind the scenes.</p>
        <p><strong>Self-hosted.</strong> Deploy on your own infrastructure. Full control over data, security, and operations. Same platform, your servers.</p>
