        <h2>The Graduation Path for Treasury Operations</h2>
        <p>Family offices and corporate treasury groups face a recurring problem: the platforms available are either shared (no control) or require building from scratch (too expensive). CCASH solves this with a graduated approach designed for treasury operations.</p>
        <p><strong>Start on the shared platform.</strong> Multi-currency wallets across USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, INR and more. Internal transfers between entities and accounts. FX and swap with quoted rates and atomic settlement — both legs post together or not at all. Risk-tiered OTP approval gates: high-value transfers require additional verification. Four-eyes control: every money movement drafted by one party, approved by another. Full ledger and reconciliation — every transaction posted, every balance verifiable.</p>
        <p><strong>Graduate when you're ready.</strong> Your own self-hosted CCASH instance. Your brand. Your infrastructure. Your data. Full cryptographic audit trails, multi-signature controls, and the same platform you've been using — now under your complete control. We handle the migration.</p>

        <p><strong>Multi-entity cash management.</strong> Hold balances across entities in multiple currencies. Transfer internally with approval gates. Every movement posted to the ledger.</p>
