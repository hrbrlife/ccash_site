## Community Governance

Governance defines who makes decisions in your token economy and how those decisions are made. CCASH supports a range of governance models — from simple founder-led to full token-weighted democracy — that evolve as your community grows.

### Founder-Led Governance

In the simplest model, you control the rules. Token holders get access and utility, but you make decisions about the token economy's direction, parameters, and evolution. This is the fastest way to launch and appropriate for early-stage communities.

**Best for:** Individual creators, early-stage communities, testing a token model.

### Council Governance

A group of trusted community members shares decision-making authority. Council members may be elected by token holders, appointed by the founder, or selected through a hybrid process. Each council member gets one vote.

**Best for:** Sports clubs with fan representatives, brand communities with super-user groups, growing communities that want stakeholder input.

### Token-Weighted Governance

Every token holder votes on proposals, with voting power proportional to tokens held or staked. This is the most decentralized model. Proposals can cover anything from token parameters to community treasury allocation.

**Best for:** Mature communities, DAO-like structures, communities that have graduated to self-hosted instances.

### Governance on CCASH

CCASH provides governance tooling at every stage:

- **On the shared platform:** Simple governance tools — proposals, voting, parameter changes — managed through CCASH's infrastructure
- **After graduation:** The same governance tools run on your own instance, with full control over governance parameters and upgrade paths

### Graduating Governance

When you graduate from the shared platform to your own self-hosted instance, your governance structures graduate with you. Your proposal history, voting records, and governance parameters transfer cleanly. You can evolve your governance model without disruption.
