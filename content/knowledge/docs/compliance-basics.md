## Compliance for Money-Services Operations

When you launch a money-services operation, you take on compliance obligations — identity verification, transaction review, and regulatory reporting. CCASH provides operator-facing compliance surfaces that integrate with dedicated compliance infrastructure.

### Identity Verification

Every participant in your operation should be verified. CCASH provides operator-facing KYC/KYB intake and review screens that you can tune to your client base's needs:

- **Basic verification** — Email and identity document. Appropriate for low-value operations.
- **Standard verification** — Identity document upload plus biometric verification (via integrated identity verification infrastructure). Appropriate for standard money-services operations.
- **Enhanced verification** — Full KYC with source of funds, background checks. Required for higher-value operations and higher-risk verticals.

### Transaction Review

The platform surfaces transaction monitoring verdicts from the integrated compliance infrastructure. Flagged transactions appear in the operator compliance dashboard for review and action according to your procedures. On the shared platform, the compliance surfaces are provided as part of the platform. After graduation, you run the same surfaces on your own instance.

### Reporting Obligations

Depending on your jurisdiction and operation type, you may have reporting obligations — suspicious activity reports (SARs), currency transaction reports (CTRs), or other filings. CCASH provides SAR/STR drafting and filing surfaces, along with the audit trail and data you need to meet your obligations.

### Compliance Graduates Too

When you graduate to your own self-hosted CCASH instance, your compliance records graduate with you. Your verification records, transaction history, and audit trail transfer cleanly. No re-onboarding, no compliance gap, no data loss.
