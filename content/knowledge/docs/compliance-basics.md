## Compliance for Token Economies

When you launch a community token economy, you take on compliance obligations — identity verification, transaction monitoring, and regulatory reporting. CCASH handles the compliance infrastructure so you can focus on your community.

### Identity Verification

Every participant in your token economy should be verified. CCASH provides configurable KYC/KYB workflows that you can tune to your community's needs:

- **Basic verification** — Email and wallet address. Appropriate for low-value access tokens.
- **Standard verification** — Identity document upload + liveness check. Appropriate for membership and loyalty tokens.
- **Enhanced verification** — Full KYC with source of funds, background checks. Required for higher-value token economies.

### Transaction Monitoring

CCASH monitors token transactions in real time for suspicious patterns. Flagged transactions are reviewed according to your compliance procedures. On the shared platform, CCASH handles monitoring. After graduation, you run the same monitoring on your own instance.

### Reporting Obligations

Depending on your jurisdiction and token model, you may have reporting obligations — suspicious activity reports, currency transaction reports, or other regulatory filings. CCASH provides the audit trail and data you need to meet these obligations.

### Compliance Graduates Too

When you graduate to your own self-hosted CCASH instance, your compliance infrastructure graduates with you. Your verification records, transaction history, and audit trail transfer cleanly. No re-onboarding, no compliance gap, no data loss.
