## Launching Your Community Token

Launching a community token on CCASH follows a simple path. You don't need to be a blockchain developer or have any infrastructure experience. CCASH handles the technical complexity so you can focus on your community.

### Step 1: Choose Your Model

What kind of token economy fits your community?

- **Fan Token** — for celebrities, musicians, and sports clubs. Token-gated exclusive content, fan voting, meet-and-greet access, digital collectibles.
- **Loyalty Token** — for brands, shopping centres, and retail destinations. Cross-tenant rewards, visitor engagement, repeat-customer incentives.
- **Membership Token** — for clubs, organisations, and membership communities. Access gating, governance rights, tiered membership.
- **Community Token** — for creators, influencers, and audience-driven projects. Paid subscriptions, exclusive channels, community rewards.

### Step 2: Start on the Shared Platform

You launch on CCASH's shared infrastructure. No servers to set up, no blockchain to configure, no compliance program to build from scratch. CCASH handles:

- Token creation and smart contract deployment
- Identity verification for your community members
- Transaction monitoring and compliance
- Audit trail generation

### Step 3: Build Your Community

Once your token is live, you focus on growing your community. The platform provides tools for:

- Token distribution and management
- Member onboarding and verification
- Analytics and insights
- Community governance

### Step 4: Graduate to Self-Hosted

When your community reaches the scale where you want full control, you graduate to your own self-hosted CCASH instance. Your token data, community records, and audit history transfer cleanly. Your brand, your infrastructure, your terms.

### Next Steps

Contact the CCASH team to discuss your token economy model and start the onboarding process.
