## Token Economy Design

A community token economy is more than just a token — it's the set of incentives, utilities, and governance structures that create value for your community. This guide covers the key design decisions you'll make when launching on CCASH.

### Token Utility

Your token needs a reason to exist. What can holders do with it?

- **Access** — Token gates exclusive content, channels, events, or experiences
- **Voting** — Token holders vote on community decisions, content direction, or resource allocation
- **Rewards** — Tokens are earned through participation and redeemed for perks
- **Governance** — Token holders propose and vote on changes to the community rules
- **Exchange** — Tokens can be traded, transferred, or used within your community ecosystem

### Distribution Model

How do tokens enter your community?

- **Launch distribution** — Initial allocation to founding members, early supporters, or existing audience
- **Earned distribution** — Tokens earned through participation, content creation, or community contribution
- **Purchased distribution** — Tokens available for purchase by community members
- **Reward distribution** — Tokens as incentives for specific behaviors or milestones

### Governance Structure

Who decides how the token economy evolves?

- **Founder-led** — You control the rules and parameters. Simple, fast, and appropriate for early stages.
- **Council-based** — A group of trusted community members share governance. Balanced control.
- **Token-weighted** — All token holders vote on changes. Full decentralization. Appropriate for mature communities.

### Growth Mechanics

How does your token economy attract and retain members?

- **Earn mechanics** — Members earn tokens through participation, creating a virtuous cycle
- **Burn mechanics** — Tokens consumed when used, creating scarcity and ongoing demand
- **Staking mechanics** — Members lock tokens to access premium features or earn rewards
- **Referral mechanics** — Members earn tokens by bringing new members into the community

### Graduation Readiness

Design your token economy from day one with graduation in mind. Choose models that can scale to your own infrastructure. CCASH supports you at every stage — from shared platform launch to self-hosted graduation.
