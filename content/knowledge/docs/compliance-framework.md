    <h2>BSA/AML Program</h2>
    <p>Risk-based AML program under 31 CFR §1022.210. AI-assisted transaction monitoring, sanctions screening, KYC/KYB, SAR filing, and OFAC compliance. Four-eyes approval on every critical action.</p>

    <h2>Client Series Agent MSB</h2>
    <p>Each Series operates as a designated agent under 31 CFR §1022.380(d). Recorded on CCA.SH's internal agent list, maintained at a U.S. location, revised annually. No separate FinCEN registration needed during tenancy.</p>

    <h2>Four-Eyes Operations</h2>
    <p>High-tier transactions, KYC final approval, sanctions-hit overrides, refunds above threshold, and SAR filings all require two distinct signers. On-chain signature verification through Melusina OS identity gate.</p>
