    <h2>Understanding the Model</h2>
    <p>CCA.SH is a FinCEN-registered Money Services Business organized as a Montana Series LLC. The core product is the Whitelabel Partner Series — a branded, regulated MSB, PSP, or VASP operation that launches under CCA.SH's principal MSB registration with a defined path to independence.</p>

    <h2>Key Concepts</h2>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Partner LLC:</strong> Your standalone Montana LLC, which you own and control. Holds your brand, domain, and operating manuals.</li>
        <li><strong>Series C-###:</strong> A dedicated cell within CCA.SH's Master Series LLC. Holds your customers, records, wallets, and accounts.</li>
        <li><strong>Agent MSB:</strong> Your Series operates as a designated agent under 31 CFR §1022.380(d) — no separate FinCEN registration needed during tenancy.</li>
        <li><strong>Graduation:</strong> A 90-day planned path to your own independent FinCEN registration and state MTLs.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>Onboarding Process</h2>
    <p>The full onboarding follows a 13-gate process: initial inquiry and NDA, preliminary eligibility assessment, detailed application, KYC/KYB verification, risk assessment, Partner LLC formation, Series creation, correspondent sub-account provisioning, Fineract Tenant setup, wallet provisioning, compliance training, Client Services Agreement execution, and go-live.</p>
