    <h2>Understanding the CCASH Model</h2>
    <p>CCASH provides a complete legal infrastructure and technology platform for regulated financial services. The platform operates on a dual-layer model: a legal infrastructure layer providing the entity structure, governance, and compliance framework, and a technology platform layer providing the Go + HTMX application stack, secure data layer, and deployment tooling.</p>

    <h2>Key Concepts</h2>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Legal Infrastructure:</strong> The entity structure, governance framework, and compliance policies that form the regulatory foundation of your operation.</li>
        <li><strong>Technology Platform:</strong> The Go + HTMX application framework, encrypted SQLite data layer, and deployment tooling that powers your services.</li>
        <li><strong>Operator Model:</strong> Operators deploy branded instances of the CCASH technology platform within the CCASH legal framework, serving their own customers under their own brand.</li>
        <li><strong>Scaling:</strong> The growth path from initial deployment to full-scale operation on the CCASH platform.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>Accessing the Legal Framework Documentation</h2>
    <p>The legal framework documentation covers entity structure, governance policies, compliance procedures, and operational agreements. All documentation is available through the knowledge base and is organized by category for easy reference.</p>

    <h2>Deploying the Go + HTMX Application Platform</h2>
    <p>The CCASH technology platform is built with Go and HTMX, providing a modern, lightweight application stack. Deployment follows a straightforward process: clone the platform repository, configure your brand profile, set up the encrypted SQLite data layer, and deploy via the included tooling. Detailed deployment instructions are available in the platform documentation.</p>

    <h2>Configuring Your Brand Profile</h2>
    <p>Each operator configures their brand profile, including branding assets, domain configuration, and operational preferences. The brand profile is stored in the encrypted data layer and controls the customer-facing experience.</p>
