## Self-Hosting Guide

Graduating to your own self-hosted CCASH instance is the final step in the graduation journey. This guide covers what to expect, how to prepare, and what transfers.

### When to Graduate

You're ready to graduate when:

- Your operation has reached a size where shared-platform fees exceed self-hosting costs
- You want full control over your infrastructure, data, and security practices
- You need custom features or integrations that require direct platform access
- You want to eliminate any dependency on third-party infrastructure

### What Transfers

When you graduate, the following transfers to your self-hosted instance:

- **Client records** — Client profiles, verification status, and participation history
- **Transaction data** — Wallet balances, transaction history, and platform state
- **Audit trail** — Every action logged and cryptographically signed, fully replayable
- **Governance records** — Proposal history, voting records, and governance parameters
- **Compliance records** — Verification data, monitoring logs, and reporting history

### What You Need

To self-host, you need:

- A Linux server (bare metal, VM, or cloud instance) meeting CCASH's system requirements
- Access to a supported blockchain network for money-services operations
- Your own domain and SSL certificate
- Basic DevOps capability or willingness to engage CCASH's deployment support

### The Graduation Process

1. **Preparation** — CCASH provides your graduation kit: deployment scripts, configuration templates, and migration tools
2. **Deployment** — Your self-hosted instance is deployed and verified
3. **Migration** — Your client data, transaction records, and audit history are exported from the shared platform
4. **Verification** — Everything is checked for integrity: balances match, audit trail is continuous, no data gaps
5. **Cutover** — Your client base transitions to the self-hosted instance

### Post-Graduation

After graduation, you own your platform. You can continue to route money-services operations through CCASH's infrastructure if needed, or operate entirely independently. You receive ongoing CCASH platform updates. You have full control over when and how to upgrade.

### Need Help?

CCASH provides deployment support for self-hosting. Contact the team for assistance with your graduation.
