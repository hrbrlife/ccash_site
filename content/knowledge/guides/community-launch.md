## Launching Your Token to Your Community

You've chosen your token model and deployed on CCASH. Now it's time to launch to your community. A successful launch turns interest into participation.

### Before Launch

**Tell your community what's coming.** Announce the token economy before it launches. Build anticipation. Explain what the token does, why it matters, and how to get it.

**Set expectations.** Be clear about what the token enables — access, voting, rewards — and what it doesn't do. Transparency builds trust.

**Prepare your first members.** Identify your most engaged community members and give them early access. Their participation sets the tone for everyone else.

### Launch Phase

**Make it easy to join.** The simpler the onboarding, the more participants you'll get. CCASH handles identity verification and token distribution so your community members don't face technical hurdles.

**Create urgency.** Limited early-bird allocations, founder member status, or launch-only benefits drive early participation.

**Be present.** The founder or community manager should be active during launch. Answer questions, celebrate milestones, and demonstrate the token utility in real time.

### After Launch

**Show momentum.** Publicly share milestone metrics — number of token holders, transaction volume, governance participation. Momentum attracts more participants.

**Iterate on feedback.** Your community will tell you what's working and what isn't. Use governance tools to let them shape the token economy.

**Plan for growth.** As your community grows, use CCASH's scaling tools. When you outgrow the shared platform, start planning your graduation.

### What CCASH Handles

On the shared platform, CCASH handles the technical launch — token distribution, identity verification, transaction processing, and compliance monitoring. You focus on your community. When you graduate, you take over the technical operations with the same tools and workflows.
