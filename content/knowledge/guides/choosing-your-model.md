## Choosing Your Token Model

Not every community needs the same token model. This guide helps you choose the right model for your audience and goals.

### Fan Token

**Best for:** Celebrities, musicians, sports clubs, artists, public figures

Fan tokens create a direct economic relationship between public figures and their audience. Holders get exclusive access, voting rights, and digital collectibles. The token becomes a symbol of fandom and a gateway to the inner circle.

**Key questions to ask:**

- Do you have an audience that wants to feel closer to you?
- Can you offer exclusive content, access, or experiences?
- Do you want your fans to have a say in your creative or sporting decisions?

### Loyalty Token

**Best for:** Shopping centres, retail destinations, lifestyle brands, hospitality groups

Loyalty tokens replace traditional points-based programs with a token economy. Customers earn tokens through purchases, visits, and engagement. Tokens can be redeemed, traded, or used across a network of businesses.

**Key questions to ask:**

- Do you have repeat customers you want to reward?
- Can you create a token that works across multiple businesses in your ecosystem?
- Do you want a loyalty program that customers actually care about?

### Membership Token

**Best for:** Clubs, organisations, professional networks, membership communities

Membership tokens gate access to a community. Holding the token proves membership and enables participation. Tokens can represent tiers — basic, premium, founder — each with different access levels.

**Key questions to ask:**

- Do you have an exclusive community or club?
- Can you offer tiered access levels?
- Do you want members to have governance rights?

### Community Token

**Best for:** Creators, influencers, bloggers, audience-driven projects

Community tokens are the most flexible model. They can combine elements of fan tokens, loyalty tokens, and membership tokens. The key is that the token represents participation in and ownership of a community economy.

**Key questions to ask:**

- Do you have an engaged audience that wants more than passive consumption?
- Can you build a mini-economy around your content or brand?
- Do you want your most engaged followers to benefit from your success?
