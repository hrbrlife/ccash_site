## Designing Token Utility

Token utility is what your token actually does. Without clear utility, a token is just a number on a screen. This guide helps you design utility that drives engagement and creates lasting value.

### The Utility Spectrum

Token utility ranges from simple to sophisticated:

**Simple** — Token gates access to existing content or experiences. Low complexity, easy to implement, clear value proposition.

**Moderate** — Token enables voting, governance, or community decision-making. Requires more community buy-in but creates deeper engagement.

**Advanced** — Token is the core of a community economy — earn, spend, trade, stake, govern. Highest engagement potential but requires ongoing management.

### Utility That Works

The most effective token utilities share common patterns:

**Exclusivity.** Token holders get something non-holders don't. A private channel, early access, a limited-edition item. Scarcity drives demand.

**Voice.** Token holders influence decisions. Which content gets created? Which charity gets donations? What rules govern the community? Voice drives ownership.

**Reward.** Token holders earn benefits proportional to their participation. The more contribution, the more rewards. Virtuous cycle.

**Identity.** Token holding signals membership. A fan token in your wallet says "I'm part of this community." Status drives adoption.

### Utility to Avoid

**No utility.** A token that does nothing is a speculation vehicle, not a community tool.

**Fake utility.** Utility that sounds good but doesn't actually work in practice. Test your utility with real community members before launch.

**Overcomplicated utility.** Too many use cases confuse your community. Start simple, add complexity as your community grows.

### Iterating on Utility

Your token utility should evolve with your community. Start with simple access gating. Add voting when your community is ready. Introduce rewards when you have participation data. CCASH supports you at every stage — from shared platform to self-hosted graduation.
