KYC (Know Your Customer) is the regulatory process of verifying the identity of participants in a financial ecosystem. On CCASH, KYC workflows are built into the platform — whether you're on the shared platform or a self-hosted instance. Operators configure identity verification requirements for their community token participants based on their specific regulatory obligations and risk profile.
