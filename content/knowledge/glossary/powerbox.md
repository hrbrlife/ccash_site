title: Powerbox - Glossary
---
The Powerbox is CCASH's inter-grain capability sharing mechanism. Instead of sharing passwords or API keys, grains offer capabilities to each other through the Powerbox. Capabilities are fine-grained, revocable, and auditable — ensuring least-privilege access at every level of a CCASH deployment.
