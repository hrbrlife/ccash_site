<section class="term-section"><h2>Full Definition</h2><p>A governance requirement that certain critical actions must be approved by two distinct authorized signers before execution. Within the CCASH platform, four-eyes applies to high-tier transactions, customer verification final approval, override decisions on flagged items, refunds above threshold, and policy exception approvals. The requirement is enforced through the technology platform's cryptographic signature verification, ensuring that no single individual can authorize sensitive operations.</p></section>
