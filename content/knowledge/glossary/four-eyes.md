<section class="term-section"><h2>Full Definition</h2><p>A governance requirement that certain critical actions must be approved by two distinct authorized signers before execution. On CCA.SH, four-eyes applies to high-tier transactions, KYC final approval, sanctions overrides, SAR filings, and capital adequacy attestations.</p></section>
