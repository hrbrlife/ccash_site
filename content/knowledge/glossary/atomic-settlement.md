Atomic Settlement is the simultaneous exchange of assets and payment in a single transaction — neither side can fail without the other. On CCASH, a Swap applies the quoted FX rate and settles atomically: one balance is debited and the other credited in a single operation. Both legs post to the ledger together or not at all. This eliminates settlement risk — the window where one party has paid but the other has not.
