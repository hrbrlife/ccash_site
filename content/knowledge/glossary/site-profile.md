A Site Profile is the per-operator configuration that defines a whitelabel deployment: brand and domain, enabled features (Send/Receive/Swap, travel cheques), supported currencies and FX sources, risk tiers and approval-gate thresholds, and KYC settings. On the shared platform each Client Series has its own site profile, isolated from others, and the same profile carries over when an operator graduates to a self-hosted instance.
