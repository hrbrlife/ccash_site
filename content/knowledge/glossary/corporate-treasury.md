Corporate Treasury is the management of an organization's own cash and assets — holding balances across currencies, moving funds, converting via FX, and keeping accurate records. CCASH runs as treasury software: multi-currency wallets, Send/Receive/Swap, FX, multi-party approval gates, and reconciliation give a company controlled, auditable management of its funds, in the same platform an operator uses to serve customers.
