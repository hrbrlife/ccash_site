An FX Rate is the price at which one currency or asset is exchanged for another. CCASH applies an FX rate to every Swap and cross-currency payment, sourced and quoted at the time of the transaction. The rate used is recorded with the transaction so the conversion can be reconciled and audited, and operators can configure spreads and rate sources per deployment.
