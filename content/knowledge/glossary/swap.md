Swap is the in-wallet exchange of one currency or asset for another — fiat to stablecoin, crypto to fiat, or between currencies. On CCASH a Swap applies the quoted FX rate and settles atomically, debiting one balance and crediting the other in a single operation, with screening and approval gates applied by risk tier. Both legs are posted to the ledger and signed into the audit trail.
