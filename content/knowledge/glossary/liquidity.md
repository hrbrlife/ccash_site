Liquidity refers to the availability of funds to meet payment obligations as they fall due. In money-services operations, liquidity management involves maintaining sufficient balances across currencies and accounts to settle client transactions without delay. CCASH supports liquidity management through multi-currency wallet visibility, FX and swap with atomic settlement, and real-time balance monitoring.
