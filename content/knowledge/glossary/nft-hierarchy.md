The NFT Hierarchy is CCASH's 4-layer cryptographic chain of trust: Master NFT (root authority) → Reseller NFTs (delegated authority) → License NFTs (per-installation authority) → Share NFTs (per-domain access tokens). Every operation on the platform traces its authority back through this hierarchy. No NFT, no authority.
