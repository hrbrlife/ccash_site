A PSP (Payment Service Provider) moves money on behalf of customers — accepting funds, holding balances, and executing payments. CCASH is whitelabel PSP software: an operator runs Send, Receive, and payout flows over multi-currency wallets, with risk-tiered approval gates, KYC, and a signed audit trail built in. PSP activity sits alongside the platform's MSB and VASP capabilities for fiat, stablecoin, and crypto.
