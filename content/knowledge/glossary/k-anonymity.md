<section class="term-section"><h2>Full Definition</h2><p>A privacy protection that suppresses aggregate data in any bucket with fewer than k distinct individuals. CCA.SH applies k ≥ 25 for Whitelabel Partner Series information access to prevent inference of individual customer data from aggregate figures.</p></section>
