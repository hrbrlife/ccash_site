<section class="term-section"><h2>Full Definition</h2><p>Governance roles define the responsibilities, authorities, and limitations of each participant within the CCASH platform. The governance framework establishes supervisory roles with defined veto scope covering regulatory safety, intellectual property compliance, and operational integrity. Operational roles handle day-to-day management of services within the boundaries set by the governance framework.</p></section>
