A VASP (Virtual Asset Service Provider) handles crypto and stablecoin on behalf of customers — exchange, transfer, and custody of digital assets. CCASH provides the VASP software an operator needs to run those flows: crypto and stablecoin wallets, Swap, on-chain transfer hooks, sanctions screening, and travel-rule data capture, all recorded in the signed audit trail. VASP capabilities run together with the platform's MSB and PSP money-movement features.
