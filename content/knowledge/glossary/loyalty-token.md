A Loyalty Token reimagines traditional loyalty programs as token economies. Unlike traditional points that expire or get locked in, loyalty tokens can be traded, transferred, and governed by the community. Shopping centres, lifestyle brands, and retail destinations use CCASH to launch loyalty token programs — from cross-tenant rewards to visitor engagement.
