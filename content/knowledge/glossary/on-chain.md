On-Chain refers to data or logic that exists on a distributed ledger. CCASH uses on-chain verification mechanisms — cryptographic signatures, authority verification, and audit trails — to enforce rules at the protocol level. Every state-changing audit row carries a cryptographic signature, creating a tamper-evident record that can be independently verified.
