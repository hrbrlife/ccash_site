Multi-Party Control requires more than one authorized person to complete a sensitive action, so no single individual can act alone. On CCASH it backs high-risk approval gates and key operations — large or unusual transfers, configuration changes, and overrides — using four-eyes review and threshold signing. Each participant's approval is cryptographically signed and recorded in the audit trail.
