Fiat rails refer to the integration of a money-services operation with traditional payment and settlement infrastructure — correspondent banking, SEPA, SWIFT, ACH, and card networks. The CCASH platform supports operator-configured fiat rails through provider wiring and the Swap (FX) surface, enabling fiat on-ramps, off-ramps, and settlement across the operator's configured currency set.
