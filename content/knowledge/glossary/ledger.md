The Ledger is the authoritative record of balances and movements across every wallet on a CCASH deployment. Each Send, Receive, Swap, and travel-cheque action posts double-entry records to the ledger, written append-only to the journal and cryptographically signed. The ledger is the source of truth that reconciliation, reporting, and the audit trail are built on.
