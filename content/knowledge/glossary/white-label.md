White-Label refers to operating a CCASH platform under your own brand. On the shared platform, your community sees your brand while CCASH provides the infrastructure. When you graduate to self-hosted, your brand operates on your own infrastructure. Your community never needs to know about CCASH unless you choose to tell them.
