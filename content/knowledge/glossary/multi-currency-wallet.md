A Multi-Currency Wallet holds balances in more than one currency or asset class — fiat, stablecoin, and crypto — under a single customer account. On CCASH each wallet supports Send, Receive, and Swap across its balances, with custody, screening, and approval gates applied per transaction. It is the core customer-facing object an operator's PSP, MSB, and VASP flows act on.
