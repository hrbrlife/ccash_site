A Broker-Dealer is a financial intermediary that buys and sells securities on behalf of clients or its own account. Broker-dealers are registered with the SEC and FINRA in the United States. CCASH is not a broker-dealer — it is a money-services platform operated by a FinCEN-registered MSB. Clients who are broker-dealers may use CCASH infrastructure for their money-services operations.
