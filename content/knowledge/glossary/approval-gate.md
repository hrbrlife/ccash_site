An Approval Gate is a risk-tiered checkpoint that holds a transaction until the required authorization is given. CCASH classifies actions by risk and routes higher-risk ones — large transfers, swaps, travel-cheque redemptions, overrides — through OTP confirmation and, where configured, multi-party approval before they execute. Every gate decision is captured in the signed audit trail.
