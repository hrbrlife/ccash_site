A Fan Token is a type of community token used by celebrities, musicians, and sports clubs to connect directly with their audience. Fan tokens can gate exclusive content, voting rights, meet-and-greet access, digital collectibles, and more. Launch on CCASH's shared platform. Graduate to your own branded instance when your fan economy grows.
