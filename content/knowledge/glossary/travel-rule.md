The Travel Rule requires that originator and beneficiary information accompany qualifying funds and virtual-asset transfers between providers. CCASH captures and transmits the required counterparty data on Send and crypto transfers, ties it to KYC records, and logs it in the signed audit trail — so an operator running MSB, PSP, and VASP flows can meet travel-rule obligations as part of normal money movement.
