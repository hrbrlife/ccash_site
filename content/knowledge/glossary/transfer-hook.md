title: Transfer Hook - Glossary

A Transfer Hook is an on-chain compliance mechanism that executes before any token transfer. The hook checks sender authorization, receiver eligibility, volume limits, and jurisdiction filters — denying non-compliant transfers at the protocol level. This ensures compliance is enforced, not just monitored.
