Settlement Instructions are the details that tell the platform how and where to move funds to complete a payment — the paying and receiving accounts or wallets, currency, amount, rail, and any references. On CCASH each Send, Swap, or payout carries settlement instructions that drive the ledger postings and feed reconciliation, so every movement can be matched against what actually settled.
