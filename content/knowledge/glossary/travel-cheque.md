A Travel Cheque is a prepaid, redeemable instrument an operator can issue and later honor for value. On CCASH a customer can buy a travel cheque from a wallet balance and redeem it later; issuance and redemption pass through risk-tiered approval gates and screening, post to the ledger, and are recorded in the signed audit trail. Buy and redeem are tracked so outstanding cheques reconcile against issued value.
