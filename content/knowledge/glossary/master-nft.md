The Master NFT is the root authority in CCASH's 4-layer NFT hierarchy. Controlled by threshold signing (requiring multiple key holders to agree), the Master NFT sits at the top of the chain of trust and can issue Reseller NFTs. It represents the sovereign authority for a CCASH deployment.
