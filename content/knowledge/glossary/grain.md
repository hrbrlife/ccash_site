title: Grain - Glossary

A Grain is the fundamental isolation unit in the Sandstorm/Melusina operating system. Think of it as an indestructible, self-contained process that remembers everything. Each grain has its own append-only journal store, a set of capabilities granted to it, and an independent lifecycle. In CCASH, grains form the building blocks of both the shared platform and self-hosted instances.
