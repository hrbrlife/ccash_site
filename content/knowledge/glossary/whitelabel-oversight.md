Whitelabel Oversight is the supervision CCASH retains over operators running on the shared platform under its US FinCEN MSB registration. While a Client Series operates as an Agent MSB under its own brand, CCASH monitors compliance posture, approval-gate activity, and the signed audit trail across tenants. Oversight applies until an operator graduates to a self-hosted instance or its own license, after which it operates independently.
