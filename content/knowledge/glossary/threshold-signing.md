title: Threshold Signing - Glossary
---
Threshold Signing is a cryptographic scheme where a minimum number of independent signers must cooperate to produce a valid signature. No single party can act alone. CCASH uses threshold signing for high-authority operations — Master NFT control, governance decisions, and critical system parameters.
