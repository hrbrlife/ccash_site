<section class="term-section"><h2>Full Definition</h2><p><strong>FinCEN</strong> (Financial Crimes Enforcement Network) is a bureau of the US Department of the Treasury that administers the Bank Secrecy Act (BSA). FinCEN regulates Money Services Businesses (MSBs) under 31 CFR §1022.380, requiring registration, AML program implementation, recordkeeping, and reporting of suspicious activity. CCA.SH MONEY SERVICES (US) SERIES LLC is a FinCEN-registered MSB. The CCASH platform's compliance surfaces are designed to support operations conducted under FinCEN-registered MSB structures.</p></section>
