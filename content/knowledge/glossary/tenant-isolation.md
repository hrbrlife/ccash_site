Tenant Isolation keeps each operator's data, balances, and configuration separate on the shared platform, so one tenant cannot see or affect another. CCASH enforces this through capability-secured grains and per-tenant journals: every operation is scoped to its own tenant by construction. Isolation underpins whitelabel hosting and makes graduation to a dedicated self-hosted instance a clean handover.
