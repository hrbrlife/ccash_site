A Governance Framework defines how decisions are made within a money-services operation — who can propose changes, how approvals work, and what authority different participants have. CCASH provides governance tooling including role-based access control (7 user roles), multi-party approval for critical operations, and cryptographically signed audit trails. The framework travels with you — from the shared platform through self-hosted graduation.
