The Shared Platform is CCASH's multi-tenant infrastructure where community token economies launch before they graduate to self-hosted instances. It provides banking, compliance, and technology infrastructure that every participant shares — meaning you can launch your token economy in days, not months, without building any infrastructure yourself.
