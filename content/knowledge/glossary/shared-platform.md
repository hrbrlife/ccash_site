The Shared Platform is CCASH's operator infrastructure where money-services businesses launch as a Client Series (C-###) before they graduate to self-hosted instances. It provides compliance surfaces and technology infrastructure — one grain per client — meaning you can launch your operation in weeks, not months, without building infrastructure yourself. Each client operates under CCASH's US FinCEN MSB registration as an Agent MSB, with the path to graduate to their own self-hosted instance (Path A) or own license (Path B).
