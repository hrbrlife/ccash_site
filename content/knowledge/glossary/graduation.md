<section class="term-section"><h2>Full Definition</h2><p>Scaling refers to the process of growing an operation on the CCASH platform from initial deployment to full-scale operation. As operators grow their customer base, transaction volume, and operational footprint, they can expand their use of the CCASH legal infrastructure and technology platform. The platform is designed to accommodate growth without requiring operators to rebuild their infrastructure.</p></section>
