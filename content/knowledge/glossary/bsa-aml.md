<section class="term-section"><h2>Full Definition</h2><p>The compliance framework within the CCASH platform consists of policies and procedures covering customer identification and verification, customer due diligence, transaction review, and reporting obligations. The platform provides operator-facing review surfaces for sanctions screening verdicts and transaction monitoring — the screening engine runs in dedicated compliance infrastructure integrated with the platform. These policies form the foundation of the compliance layer and are enforced through the technology platform's governance tooling.</p></section>
