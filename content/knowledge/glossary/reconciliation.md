Reconciliation is the process of matching the platform's ledger against what actually settled on external rails and custody accounts, and resolving any differences. On CCASH ledger postings, settlement instructions, and FX rates give each movement the data needed to reconcile automatically, with exceptions surfaced for review. Reconciliation keeps balances accurate and supports treasury reporting and audit.
