AML (Anti-Money Laundering) refers to the laws, regulations, and procedures that prevent criminals from disguising illegally obtained funds as legitimate income. CCASH provides AML compliance infrastructure — transaction monitoring, sanctions screening, and suspicious activity reporting — for every community token economy on the platform.
