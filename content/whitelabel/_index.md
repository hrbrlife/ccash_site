        <h2>The Two-Entity Structure</h2>
        <p>From day one, every whitelabel partner is established with a two-entity skeleton designed for both operational safety and eventual independence.</p>

        <div class="concept-box">
            <h4>Your Partner LLC</h4>
            <p>A standalone Montana LLC that <strong>you own and control</strong>. It holds your brand, your domain names, your trade names, and your operating manuals. CCA.SH holds a Manager's Delegate supervisory role with an enumerated veto scope limited to regulatory safety — outside those areas, your control is unconditional.</p>
        </div>

        <div class="concept-box">
            <h4>Your Series C-###</h4>
            <p>A dedicated cell within CCA.SH's Master Series LLC. It holds your customers, transaction records, segregated group accounts, dedicated Fineract Tenant, segregated hot and cold crypto wallets, and per-Whitelabel-Client correspondent sub-accounts. You hold an advisor seat with aggregate information rights.</p>
        </div>
