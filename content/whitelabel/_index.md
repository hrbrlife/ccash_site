<section class="content-section">
    <div class="content-container">
        <h2>The Two-Entity Model</h2>
        <p>From day one, every operator is established with a two-entity structure designed for both operational integrity and growth.</p>

        <div class="concept-box">
            <h4>Your Operating Entity</h4>
            <p>A standalone legal entity that <strong>you own and control</strong>. It holds your brand, your domain names, your trade names, and your operating manuals. CCASH holds a supervisory governance role with an enumerated scope limited to regulatory and platform integrity — outside those areas, your control is unconditional.</p>
        </div>

        <div class="concept-box">
            <h4>Your Entity Compartment</h4>
            <p>A dedicated compartment within the CCASH legal infrastructure. It holds your customers, transaction records, segregated accounts, and data. You hold information rights covering your compartment's operations and aggregate platform data.</p>
        </div>

        <h3>Legal Documentation</h3>
        <p>The legal framework includes comprehensive documentation covering entity formation, governance policies, compliance procedures, and operational agreements. All documentation is maintained within the CCASH platform and updated as regulatory requirements evolve.</p>

        <h3>Compliance Policies</h3>
        <p>The CCASH compliance framework provides policy infrastructure covering customer identification and verification, due diligence, transaction monitoring, and reporting. Policies are enforced through the technology platform's governance tooling.</p>

        <h3>Technology Platform Deployment</h3>
        <p>The CCASH technology platform — built with Go and HTMX — provides the application layer for your operation. Deployment includes your branded instance with encrypted data storage, cryptographic security, and integrated compliance tooling.</p>
    </div>
</section>
