        <h2>The Thesis</h2>
        <p>Every regulated financial service needs two things that are rarely built together: a sound legal structure with proper entity governance, and a modern application platform that enables rapid, secure development. The former is typically handled by law firms producing static documents; the latter by engineering teams building from scratch. Neither is sufficient alone.</p>
        <p>CCASH bridges this gap. The CCASH model is two layers: a complete legal entity framework with plain-English documentation, integrated with a full Go + HTMX application platform. The legal framework defines the rules; the platform enforces them in code. Together they form a single, coherent operating system for regulated services.</p>

        <h2>The Entity Framework</h2>
        <p><strong>CCASH</strong> provides a comprehensive legal infrastructure — entity formation, governance documents, compliance procedures, and counterparty agreements — written in plain English and structured for practical use. Every document is designed to be read, understood, and operated against, not filed away.</p>

        <h2>The Technology Platform</h2>
        <p>The CCASH application platform is built with Go and HTMX, delivering a modern, high-performance web application environment. Isolated tenants, signed writes, tamper-evident audit trails, and capability-based security are properties of the architecture, enforced at every layer.</p>
