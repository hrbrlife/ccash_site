        <h2>What CCASH Is</h2>
        <p>CCASH is a whitelabel platform for Money Services Businesses (MSBs), Payment Service Providers (PSPs), Virtual Asset Service Providers (VASPs), and corporate-treasury operations. The legal entity — CCA.SH MONEY SERVICES (US) SERIES LLC — is a Montana Series LLC registered with the US Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN) as a Money Services Business under 31 CFR §1022.380.</p>
        <p>The technology platform is a mobile-first operator console (Go + HTMX, encrypted SQLite at rest) that provides multi-currency wallets, payments, foreign exchange, travel cheques, and full compliance operations. One binary boots as any operator brand via a site profile — same platform, different brand.</p>

        <h2>Our Model</h2>
        <p>CCASH operates on a simple principle: operators should not have to build infrastructure from scratch to get started, and they should not be locked in once they grow. Every client launches as a Client Series — a dedicated compartment within the CCASH legal infrastructure — operating as an Agent MSB under CCASH's FinCEN registration. When the operation scales, the client graduates: deploy a self-hosted CCASH instance on their own infrastructure (Path A), or obtain their own FinCEN MSB registration and licenses (Path B).</p>
        <p>No lock-in. No platform dependency. Your brand, your clients, your data — always.</p>

        <h2>Jurisdiction</h2>
        <p>CCASH operates under US federal and Montana law. FinCEN MSB registration provides the federal money-services framework; the Montana Series LLC provides the entity structure. All compliance, entity, and regulatory documentation is maintained within this jurisdiction.</p>
