        <h2>The Thesis</h2>
        <p>The compliance properties regulated operators need — isolated tenants, signed writes, single-use settlement, tamper-evident audit, and a defensible agent-of-principal structure — are properties of <strong>architecture</strong>, not policy. They're either structurally enforced or they're not.</p>
        <p>So we drew the smallest possible set of rules — Montana Series LLC, principal MSB, agent-list designation, four-eyes, sailor-beware, graduation — tested them into the operating documents, and built the platform under them. The Series is the commercial object. Everything else is downstream.</p>

        <h2>The Entity</h2>
        <p><strong>CCA.SH Money Services (US) Series LLC</strong> is a Montana Series LLC registered with FinCEN as an MSB under 31 CFR §1022.380. It is managed by <strong>HRBR Support Services LLC</strong>, a Montana LLC external to the Company, under a Management Services Agreement.</p>

        <h2>The Commercial Model</h2>
        <p>The main CCA.SH LLC has no direct retail or corporate clients. Its counterparties are its own Whitelabel Partner Series (C-###), banking and Partner MSB partners, vendors, and regulators. Each Partner Series operates its own brand and serves its own customers under CCA.SH's regulated infrastructure.</p>
        <p>Pricing is cost-plus and scoped per Series. Every fee shows its basis.</p>
