        <h2>Entity Structure</h2>
        <p>CCA.SH MONEY SERVICES (US) SERIES LLC is a Montana Series LLC (Montana Code Annotated Title 35, Chapter 8; series provisions under §35-8-304) registered with the US Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN) as a Money Services Business under 31 CFR §1022.380. The Series LLC structure provides segregated compartments for each whitelabel client operation.</p>

        <h2>Regulatory Basis</h2>
        <p>CCASH operates exclusively under US federal and Montana law. The FinCEN MSB registration provides the federal money-services framework. State-specific money-transmitter licenses are obtained separately where a particular product or partner requires them. CCASH does not hold foreign licenses, does not target EU/EEA residents, and does not incorporate foreign regulatory frameworks.</p>

        <h2>Compliance Program</h2>
        <p>The CCASH compliance program covers Bank Secrecy Act / Anti-Money Laundering (BSA/AML) compliance, Know Your Customer / Know Your Business (KYC/KYB) verification, sanctions screening, Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) filing, and Travel Rule compliance. Every whitelabel client operates under this program while on the CCASH shared platform and may transition to their own program upon graduation.</p>
