    <span class="section-label">Compliance</span>
    <h1 class="section-title">Built on a regulatory foundation</h1>
    <p class="section-desc">CCA.SH Money Services (US) Series LLC is a FinCEN-registered Money Services Business under 31 CFR §1022.380, organized as a Montana Series LLC. Our BSA/AML program operates under 31 CFR §1022.210.</p>
</section>

<section class="content-section">
    <div class="content-container">
        <h2>BSA/AML Program</h2>
        <p>Led by our Chief Compliance Officer, our compliance infrastructure covers:</p>
        <ul>
            <li><strong>Customer Identification and Verification (CIP)</strong> — risk-based identity verification</li>
            <li><strong>Customer Due Diligence (CDD)</strong> — beneficial ownership identification and profiling</li>
            <li><strong>Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)</strong> — for higher-risk profiles and verticals</li>
            <li><strong>Transaction Monitoring</strong> — AI-assisted real-time monitoring</li>
            <li><strong>Sanctions Screening</strong> — OFAC and global list screening on every transaction</li>
            <li><strong>Suspicious Activity Reporting (SAR)</strong> — timely filing with FinCEN</li>
        </ul>

        <h2>Client Series Agent MSB Status</h2>
        <p>Each Client Series (C-###) operates as a designated agent under 31 CFR §1022.380(d), recorded on CCA.SH's internal agent list maintained at a U.S. location and revised annually. No separate FinCEN registration is needed during tenancy.</p>

        <h2>Four-Eyes Governance</h2>
        <p>The following actions require <strong>two distinct signers</strong> with on-chain signature verification:</p>
        <ul>
            <li>High-tier transactions above threshold</li>
            <li>KYC final approval</li>
            <li>Sanctions-hit overrides</li>
            <li>Refunds and reversals above threshold</li>
            <li>SAR filings (CCO + CEO)</li>
            <li>Capital adequacy attestations</li>
            <li>AML program annual approval</li>
        </ul>

        <div class="concept-box">
            <h4>Dispute Resolution Waterfall</h4>
            <p>All disputes resolve through a mandatory four-stage process: Customer Support → 90-day good-faith resolution → AAA AI-assisted arbitration → AAA human arbitration fallback. Class-action and jury waivers apply. Regulator complaints preserved.</p>
        </div>

        <h2>Liability Separation</h2>
        <p>Montana Series LLC statute (MCA §35-8-304) creates legally separate compartments. Each Series maintains separate books, records, and accounts. The debts of one Series cannot reach the assets of another.</p>

        <h2>Legal Conduit, Not Moral Temple</h2>
        <p>CCA.SH screens for risk — money laundering, terror finance, sanctions, fraud — not for respectability. Lawful high-risk verticals within our stated appetite include regulated gambling, forex, adult content, crypto exchanges/VASPs, cannabis-adjacent businesses, firearms dealers, and nutraceuticals.</p>
    </div>
