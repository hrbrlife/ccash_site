        <h2>Compliance for Money-Services Operations</h2>
        <p>The CCASH compliance framework covers every stage of the graduation journey:</p>
        <ul>
            <li><strong>Customer Identification and Verification</strong> — risk-based identity intake and verification review for your clients</li>
            <li><strong>Customer Due Diligence</strong> — operator-facing Know Your Business (KYB) intake and dossier review</li>
            <li><strong>Transaction Review &amp; Suspicious Activity Reporting (SAR)</strong> — operator-facing review screens for transaction monitoring verdicts, with Suspicious Activity Report / Suspicious Transaction Report (SAR/STR) drafting and filing</li>
            <li><strong>Sanctions &amp; Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Screening Integration</strong> — the platform surfaces sanctions screening verdicts for operator review; screening runs in dedicated compliance infrastructure</li>
            <li><strong>Audit Trail</strong> — every action cryptographically signed, from shared platform to self-hosted</li>
        </ul>

        <h2>Shared Platform Compliance</h2>
        <p>When you launch on the CCASH shared platform, compliance surfaces are included. Your operation runs with access to identity review screens, transaction review workflows, and the cryptographically signed audit trail. The platform surfaces compliance verdicts for operator review and action. You focus on building your operation.</p>

        <h2>Self-Hosted Compliance</h2>
        <p>When you graduate to your own CCASH instance, the compliance framework graduates with you. Your audit history, your client verification data, your compliance records — everything transfers to your self-hosted instance. No data loss, no compliance gap, no re-onboarding your clients. Your compliance framework, now under your complete control.</p>

        <h2>Four-Eyes Governance</h2>
        <p>The following actions require <strong>two distinct signers</strong> with cryptographic signature verification:</p>
        <ul>
            <li>High-value transactions above threshold</li>
            <li>Member verification final approval</li>
            <li>Flagged-item override decisions</li>
            <li>Policy exception approvals</li>
        </ul>

        <h2>Data Protection</h2>
        <p>Whether on the shared platform or self-hosted, all data is encrypted at rest using per-operator keys. Access controls are enforced at the application layer, and all data access is logged and auditable. When you graduate, your data comes with you — encrypted, intact, and under your control.</p>
