        <h2>Data Collection and Use</h2>
        <p>CCA.SH Money Services (US) Series LLC collects and processes personal information as necessary to provide regulated money services, comply with BSA/AML obligations, and maintain regulatory compliance. Information collected includes customer identification data, transaction records, and compliance-related documentation.</p>

        <h2>Data Sharing</h2>
        <p>Customer information may be shared with banking partners, Partner MSBs, and regulators as required by law, regulation, or contract. Higher-risk indicators may trigger expedited disclosure. CCA.SH does not sell personal information to third parties.</p>

        <h2>Data Protection</h2>
        <p>Technical and organizational measures are maintained to protect personal information, including least-privilege access, encryption, and audit logging. CCA.SH's full privacy and data protection policy is set out in Document B8 of the legal filing package.</p>
