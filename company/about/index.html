Legal infrastructure meets modern technology.

CCASH exists at the intersection of legal infrastructure and software engineering. We provide the entity framework and the application platform that regulated financial services need to operate — without compromise on either side.

The Thesis

Every regulated financial service needs two things that are rarely built together: a sound legal structure with proper entity governance, and a modern application platform that enables rapid, secure development. The former is typically handled by law firms producing static documents; the latter by engineering teams building from scratch. Neither is sufficient alone.

CCASH bridges this gap. The CCASH model is two layers: a complete legal entity framework with plain-English documentation, integrated with a full Go + HTMX application platform. The legal framework defines the rules; the platform enforces them in code. Together they form a single, coherent operating system for regulated services.

    <h2>The Entity Framework</h2>
    <p><strong>CCASH</strong> provides a comprehensive legal infrastructure — entity formation, governance documents, compliance procedures, and counterparty agreements — written in plain English and structured for practical use. Every document is designed to be read, understood, and operated against, not filed away.</p>

    <h2>The Technology Platform</h2>
    <p>The CCASH application platform is built with Go and HTMX, delivering a modern, high-performance web application environment. Isolated tenants, signed writes, tamper-evident audit trails, and capability-based security are properties of the architecture, enforced at every layer.</p>
