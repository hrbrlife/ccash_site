Compliance Framework

The set of compliance policies and procedures that operators within the CCASH platform adhere to as part of the legal infrastructure.

Full Definition

The compliance framework within the CCASH platform consists of policies and procedures covering customer identification and verification, customer due diligence, transaction monitoring, sanctions screening, and reporting obligations. These policies form the foundation of the legal infrastructure layer and are enforced through the technology platform's governance tooling.
