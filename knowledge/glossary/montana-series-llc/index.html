Series LLC

An LLC structure that allows creation of legally separate compartments with segregated assets and liabilities.

Full Definition

A limited liability company structure that allows the creation of legally separate compartments within a single LLC. Each compartment has its own assets, liabilities, and purposes, providing legal separation between different operations. This structure forms the foundation of the CCASH legal infrastructure, enabling multiple operators to maintain segregated operations within a unified framework.
