Glossary

Key terms and concepts for understanding the CCASH legal infrastructure and technology platform.

Compliance Framework compliance
The set of compliance policies and procedures that operators within the CCASH platform adhere to as part of the legal infrastructure.
 Entity Compartment structure
A legally separate compartment within the CCASH legal infrastructure that holds an operator's customer records, transactions, and assets.
 Four-Eyes Principle compliance
A governance requirement that certain critical actions must be approved by two distinct authorized signers before execution.
 Framework Agreement structure
A governing instrument that establishes the terms under which an operator participates in the CCASH platform.
 Governance Roles structure
The defined roles and responsibilities within the CCASH governance framework, including supervisory and operational functions.
 K-Anonymity Floor compliance
A privacy protection that suppresses aggregate data in any bucket with fewer than k distinct individuals.
 Operating Entity structure
An operator's standalone entity within the CCASH legal infrastructure that holds their brand, intellectual property, and operational assets.
 Operator governance
An operator deploying the CCASH legal infrastructure and technology platform to serve customers under their own brand.
 Platform Disclaimer compliance
CCASH's disclosure principle that all platform services are experimental in nature and carry inherent risk. Participants accept these risks knowingly.
 Platform Ecosystem structure
The complete network of operators, legal infrastructure, technology platform, and governance framework that constitutes CCASH.
 Platform User Roles structure
The defined categories of users within the CCASH platform, including operators, their customers, and administrators.
 Regulated Operator regulatory
An operator within the CCASH platform that provides regulated financial services under the platform's legal infrastructure.
 Regulatory Framework regulatory
The body of regulations and compliance requirements that CCASH's legal infrastructure is designed to address.
 Scaling structure
The growth path from initial deployment to full-scale operation on the CCASH platform.
 Series LLC structure
An LLC structure that allows creation of legally separate compartments with segregated assets and liabilities.
