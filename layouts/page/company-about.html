Our Story

Building Money-Services Infrastructure

We're bridging the gap between modern technology and money-services compliance — making it possible for MSBs, PSPs, VASPs, and corporate treasury operators to launch on proven infrastructure and graduate to full independence.

Our Mission

Money-services businesses face a hard choice: build infrastructure from scratch (expensive, slow) or rent a platform that locks you in (no control, platform risk). CCASH solves this with a graduated approach.

We built CCASH to give operators a proven platform to start on — multi-currency wallets, payments, FX, custody, and compliance — and a clear path to their own self-hosted instance or their own MSB license when they're ready.

Our goal: make it possible for any legitimate money-services business to launch, operate, and grow — on shared infrastructure first, on their own infrastructure when they choose. No lock-in.

The CCASH Approach

We believe money-services operations need infrastructure that puts compliance and control first. CCASH provides a complete legal infrastructure and technology platform purpose-built for operators who need full sovereignty over their data, operations, and compliance.

Our platform combines pre-built Montana Series LLC legal entity frameworks with a self-hosted Go + HTMX application stack. Headquartered in Bozeman, Montana, and operating under CCASH's US FinCEN MSB registration, every whitelabel client receives a dedicated Client Series plus their own Partner LLC. Every component is isolated at the OS level via grain architecture, every interaction is mediated by authority-based capability security, and every byte of data is encrypted in append-only journal stores.

Built-in compliance workflows, document management, audit trail generation, and configurable regulatory reporting ensure your operations meet requirements from day one — on your infrastructure, under your control.
