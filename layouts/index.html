Protected by Design

Your operation.
Fully protected.

Every legal compartment is separate — if one entity has problems, others are unaffected. Your data stays within its compartment, protected by strict access controls. Four-eyes approval on every critical action with full audit trail.

Legal Separation

Montana Series LLC statute creates legally separate compartments. The debts of one Series cannot reach the assets of another. Each Series maintains separate books, records, and accounts.

Data Isolation

Each operation’s data lives in its own encrypted store. Cross-operation information access is aggregate-only with a minimum anonymity floor. Individual records require opt-in consent.

Multi-Party Controls

Critical operations require multiple independent approvers. No single person can unilaterally access funds, approve transactions, or make system changes. On-chain signature verification prevents single-point compromise.
