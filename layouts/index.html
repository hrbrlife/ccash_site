Architecture

Designed for Sovereignty

CCASH is built on a capability-secured grain architecture — every component isolated at the OS level, every interaction mediated by cryptographic authority. This is what makes the graduation model possible: your shared-platform data and your self-hosted instance run on the same secure foundation.

1

Entity Framework

CCASH provides pre-built legal entity frameworks designed for money-services operations. The Montana Series LLC architecture provides legal isolation between operating compartments while maintaining operational efficiency through a single administrative umbrella.

Series LLC architecture. Each operating compartment exists as its own Series with separate assets, liabilities, and participants — providing legal isolation while maintaining shared administration.
Dual-entity structure. Every whitelabel client gets a Client Series (C-###) within CCASH's Series LLC plus their own standalone Partner LLC — your brand, your domains, your control.
2

Compliance Integration

Operator-facing compliance surfaces for money-services operations. KYC/AML review screens, SAR/STR drafting, and Travel Rule handoff interfaces integrate with dedicated compliance infrastructure. The platform surfaces screening verdicts for operator review and action.

Operator-facing compliance. Review screens for KYC/KYB intake results, SAR/STR drafting, and Travel Rule handoff — integrating with the dedicated compliance verification and screening infrastructure.
Audit trail. Every action logged to append-only journal stores with cryptographic signatures and deterministic replay for regulatory review and disaster recovery.
3

Legal Documentation

Pre-vetted legal templates including operating agreements, client agreements, and regulatory disclosure documents. Customizable for your specific operational requirements under US federal and Montana law.

Template library. Comprehensive set of legal documents covering entity formation, governance, compliance, and operational procedures.
US federal + Montana. Frameworks designed for FinCEN MSB registration and Montana Series LLC structures, with adaptation paths for additional state money-transmitter licenses.
1

Go + HTMX Native Stack

Every component runs as a Go binary compiled to native code. No interpreter, no VM, no container overhead. HTMX delivers server-rendered HTML fragments — no SPA, no client-side routing, no JavaScript framework churn.

Native performance. Go compiled binaries with zero-copy Cap'n Proto serialization on file descriptor 3 (FD3). No HTTP bridge between components.
Server-rendered HTML. HTMX delivers interactive experiences without client-side frameworks. Every interaction authenticated at the capability level.
2

Grain Architecture

The CCASH platform uses a grain-based architecture where every component is an isolated process with its own journal store, capability set, and security boundary.

Station grains are orchestrators — long-lived components managing multiple workflows and participants across the system.
Instance grains are workers — one per entity, one per workflow, isolated and disposable. Each with its own journal store.
3

Journal-Based Storage

Every grain has an append-only journal store. Data is encrypted at rest with AES-256. Append-only logs enable deterministic replay for audit and disaster recovery. Right-to-erasure is handled via cryptographic shredding — delete the encryption key, and the data is gone.

Deterministic replay. Full state reconstruction from journal logs for audit, forensic analysis, and disaster recovery.
Retention policies. Configurable retention periods with automated purge. Seven-year default for regulatory compliance.
1

Cryptographic Authority Hierarchy

The CCASH platform enforces a 4-layer authority hierarchy — a cryptographic chain of trust from root authority to per-user credentials. Every operation traces its authority back through this hierarchy.

4-layer model. Master authority (root) → Reseller (delegated) → License (per-installation) → Share (per-domain access tokens).
No bypass. Every operation on the platform requires the appropriate cryptographic authority. No exceptions.
2

Capability Security

The Powerbox is the inter-grain capability sharing mechanism. Grains don't share passwords or API keys — they offer capabilities through the Powerbox. Capabilities are fine-grained, revocable, and auditable.

Powerbox offers and claims. Grains publish capability offers; other grains request and claim them. Claims become persistent sturdyRefs for cross-session access.
Least privilege. Each grain receives only the capabilities it needs. A payments grain gets settlement authority, not admin access. A compliance grain gets verification authority, not modification rights.
3

Defense in Depth

Every action passes through multiple authentication layers. No single layer is sufficient — all must agree before any operation proceeds. Compromise one layer and the others remain.

Wallet signature proves identity at the cryptographic level.
Authority verification proves role authorization — you must hold the right credential for the action you are requesting.
Grain isolation ensures you can only reach components you have been granted access to.
Powerbox capabilities prove inter-component authority with persistent, cross-session access.
