Solutions

Built for regulated financial operations

CCASH brings together legal infrastructure and technology into a single platform. Whether you run a payment operation, manage digital assets, oversee a family office, or build financial services technology, CCASH provides the foundation you need.

Who CCASH Serves

    <div class="concept-box">
        <h4>Legal Operations Teams</h4>
        <p>Entity formation and governance documentation, compliance policy frameworks, operational procedures, and multi-entity management structures. CCASH provides the legal scaffolding for regulated financial services without requiring each operator to build it from scratch.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="concept-box">
        <h4>Engineering Teams</h4>
        <p>A modern Go and HTMX application platform with encrypted SQLite storage, Solana Ed25519 signature security, and single-binary deployment. No JavaScript build step, no container orchestration overhead. Build and deploy financial applications quickly.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="concept-box">
        <h4>Payment Operators</h4>
        <p>Legal entity documentation for payment operations, settlement and reconciliation tools, segregated stores, and encrypted data handling. The legal framework and technology platform work together so payment operations have both structure and speed.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="concept-box">
        <h4>Family Offices</h4>
        <p>Entity management, multi-member governance structures, auditor-grade controls, and a Go and HTMX treasury management platform. Family offices gain the legal and technical infrastructure needed to manage complex financial operations across jurisdictions.</p>
    </div>

    <h2>Two Layers, One Platform</h2>
    <p>CCASH operates at two layers simultaneously. The <strong>Legal Infrastructure Layer</strong> provides entity frameworks, compliance policies, governance documentation, and operational procedures. The <strong>Technology Layer</strong> provides a Go and HTMX application platform, encrypted SQLite storage, Solana security, and single-binary deployment. Together, they form a complete operating environment for regulated financial services.</p>
</div>
