Pricing

Transparent, tiered pricing

Choose the CCASH capabilities that match your needs. Each tier covers a defined scope of legal infrastructure and technology platform services, with clear pricing and no hidden fees.

Pricing Philosophy

CCASH prices each layer of the platform independently and transparently. Clients select the capabilities they need and pay only for what they use. Every fee has a clear basis, and there are no hidden charges. Volume-based adjustments apply at higher usage levels.
