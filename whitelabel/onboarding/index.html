Onboarding

From inquiry to live operation

From initial inquiry to a live, operating CCASH platform deployment — every step verified before the next opens.

  1. Initial Inquiry & Confidentiality: Confidentiality agreement executed, preliminary discussion of your business model
  2. Eligibility Assessment: Business model review, jurisdictional check, and operational requirements analysis
  3. Detailed Application: Full business plan, operational projections, and compliance history
  4. Verification: Principal identification, beneficial ownership verification, and source of funds
  5. Risk Assessment: Risk classification and enhanced due diligence where applicable
  6. Operating Entity Formation: Legal entity created, governance agreements executed
  7. Entity Compartment Creation: Dedicated compartment established within the CCASH legal infrastructure
  8. Platform Deployment: CCASH technology platform instance provisioned and configured
  9. Brand Profile Configuration: Branding assets, domain configuration, and operational preferences set
  10. Data Layer Setup: Encrypted data storage provisioned and secured
  11. Compliance Training: Policy implementation training and operational procedure review
  12. Framework Agreement Execution: Legal agreements executed and governance roles assigned
  13. Go-Live: All steps verified, platform activated for customer service
