Privacy Policy

Data Collection and Use

CCASH collects and processes personal information as necessary to provide legal infrastructure and technology services, maintain platform security, and comply with applicable legal obligations. Information collected may include contact details, account credentials, usage data, and compliance-related documentation.

    <h2>Data Sharing</h2>
    <p>Personal information may be shared with service providers, professional advisors, and regulators as required by law, regulation, or contract. CCASH does not sell personal information to third parties.</p>

    <h2>Data Protection</h2>
    <p>Technical and organizational measures are maintained to protect personal information, including least-privilege access, encryption at rest and in transit, and comprehensive audit logging. Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis.</p>

    <h2>Data Retention</h2>
    <p>Personal information is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by applicable legal or regulatory obligations. When no longer needed, data is securely deleted or anonymized.</p>

    <h2>Your Rights</h2>
    <p>Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, delete, or port your data. Requests can be directed to legal@cca.sh.</p>

    <h2>Contact</h2>
    <p>For privacy-related inquiries, contact legal@cca.sh.</p>
